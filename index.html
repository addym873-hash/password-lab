Three rules to remember

1 · Prioritize length

A longer unpredictable password or passphrase is generally harder to guess.

2 · Avoid patterns

Names, dates, keyboard sequences, and common phrases are easy targets.

3 · Use unique passwords

Never reuse a password across accounts. Turn on multifactor authentication.
